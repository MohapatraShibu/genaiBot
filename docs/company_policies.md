# Company Policies

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work requires a stable internet connection, a dedicated workspace, and availability during core hours (10am–3pm local time). All remote work must be approved by the direct manager.

## Leave Policy
- Annual Leave: 20 days per year, accrued monthly.
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year, requires medical certificate after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers, 4 weeks for secondary caregivers.
- Public Holidays: All national public holidays are observed.

## Code of Conduct
All employees must treat colleagues with respect. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination. Report concerns to HR confidentially.

## Expense Reimbursement
Submit expense claims within 30 days of incurring the expense. Receipts are required for all claims over $25. Travel expenses must be pre-approved by your manager. Reimbursements are processed within 2 weeks.

## Data Security
Never share passwords or access credentials. Lock your screen when leaving your desk. Report any suspected security breach to IT immediately. Customer data must never be stored on personal devices.
