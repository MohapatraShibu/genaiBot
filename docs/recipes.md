# Recipes

## Classic Margherita Pizza
**Ingredients:** Pizza dough, 200g tomato sauce, 150g fresh mozzarella, fresh basil, olive oil, salt.
**Instructions:** Preheat oven to 250°C. Spread tomato sauce on dough. Add torn mozzarella. Bake 10–12 minutes until crust is golden. Top with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

## Pasta Carbonara
**Ingredients:** 400g spaghetti, 150g pancetta or guanciale, 3 eggs, 100g Pecorino Romano, black pepper.
**Instructions:** Cook pasta al dente. Fry pancetta until crispy. Mix eggs and cheese. Combine hot pasta with pancetta off heat, add egg mixture, toss quickly. Season with black pepper. Never add cream.

## Chicken Stir-Fry
**Ingredients:** 500g chicken breast, 2 cups mixed vegetables, 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, garlic, ginger.
**Instructions:** Slice chicken thinly. Heat wok on high. Cook chicken 5 minutes. Add vegetables, garlic, ginger. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Stir-fry 3–4 minutes. Serve with rice.

## Banana Smoothie
**Ingredients:** 2 ripe bananas, 1 cup milk (or almond milk), 1 tbsp honey, 1/2 cup yogurt, ice cubes.
**Instructions:** Blend all ingredients until smooth. Add more milk for thinner consistency. Serve immediately.

## Chocolate Chip Cookies
**Ingredients:** 225g butter, 200g sugar, 2 eggs, 1 tsp vanilla, 300g flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 200g chocolate chips.
**Instructions:** Cream butter and sugar. Beat in eggs and vanilla. Mix in flour and baking soda. Fold in chocolate chips. Bake at 180°C for 10–12 minutes.
